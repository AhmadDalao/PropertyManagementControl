# Property Control System Status

Updated: August 2, 2026

## Current position

The application is an operational MVP release candidate. Core property, tenant, lease, payment, maintenance, document, reporting, CMS, localization, map, permission, and audit workflows are implemented. The remaining launch work is operational configuration and business approval, not another UI rewrite.

## Working product scope

- Superadmin can manage platform users, portfolios, opening data, assets, CMS content, wording, showcase data, launch readiness, private system backups, email delivery, reports, media, and audit history. The command center also shows a bounded, deduplicated cross-portfolio activity feed that excludes showcase records and opens each affected record directly; complete history remains in Audit History.
- Owners manage their portfolio. Property managers manage only properties/buildings explicitly assigned by an owner, including their floors, units, tenants, leases, manual payments, maintenance, expenses, documents, and reports. A manager with no assignment receives no operational data.
- Tenants can see their active rental, contract period, paid and remaining amounts, PDF documents, payment history, and maintenance requests. They can attach private JPG, PNG, or WebP evidence when opening a request or add it later from the request record; owners and assigned managers see the same evidence without exposing it through the public media library.
- Owners and managers can start one guided tenancy journey: create the tenant account, continue into a prefilled draft lease, then complete a record-backed move-in checklist for the contract PDF, signed PDF, activation, and opening payment.
- Owners and assigned managers can hand off portal access from a dedicated user/tenant page. The system creates a fresh 60-minute password-setup link, revokes older reset links, blocks inactive accounts and out-of-scope managers, records a token-free audit event, and lets the recipient choose a private password without the operator retaining one.
- Owners and managers close tenancies through a controlled move-out plan. Completion requires the agreed date, returned keys, a deposit decision, termination-notice PDF, move-out inspection PDF, and preserves any final debt before releasing the asset.
- Assets support property, building, floor, unit, and space hierarchies with ownership, management, valuation, occupancy, and map coordinates.
- Owners and superadmins can create a complete building, its floors, and numbered units or commercial spaces in one guided transaction. The builder previews the generated structure, enforces active portfolio/stakeholder boundaries, and leaves the single-record form available for custom additions.
- Owners and superadmins can onboard a real portfolio from `/opening-data` using one bilingual multi-sheet XLSX workbook. Uploading creates a private, expiring preview only; duplicate codes, references, hierarchy, tenancy, currency, dates, and payment conflicts must pass validation before one atomic import creates assets, tenants, leases, installments, payments, allocations, occupancy, and an audit event. Failed imports roll back completely, and imported tenant accounts require the controlled portal-access handoff before login.
- Owners, managers, and superadmins set one property scope through a searchable, portfolio-grouped sidebar picker and carry it through dashboards, operational directories, the map, reports, queues, and XLSX exports. It scales beyond native-select limits, is session-backed, excludes inactive or unauthorized roots, clears safely when access changes, and never appears in the tenant portal or unrelated detail/system pages.
- Owners, managers, and superadmins compare their complete accessible estate in `/portfolio-control`. The bilingual card workspace ranks all properties by operating pressure, supports search, portfolio and health filters, server pagination, and opens the focused dashboard, Action Center, reports, or property record without returning to a crowded asset register.
- Lease installments distinguish current arrears from future contract balance. Payment allocation and reversal use database transactions and row locks.
- Owners and managers control rent collection through an append-only follow-up workspace. Every open installment can record the contact method, outcome, assignee, promise-to-pay, next action, and permanent history; broken promises and overdue accounts without contact feed dashboards, reports, filters, lease history, and XLSX exports.
- Owners, managers, and superadmins have one property-scoped Action Center that merges collection follow-ups, maintenance, lease renewals, move-outs, and expiring documents into a prioritized daily queue. It supports assignee, property, priority, and work-type filters plus a compact bilingual Daily Operations Brief in PDF, editable DOCX, and real XLSX formats. Every format uses the exact current filters and authorization scope, keeps unlike currencies separate, and excludes foreign properties; managers see only assigned property hierarchies and tenants cannot access it.
- Owners, assigned managers, and superadmins have one central bilingual Work Order register instead of hunting through maintenance requests or contractor profiles. It combines contractor responsibility, internal ownership, property and tenant context, visit schedule, tenant access, quoted/final costs, active/unscheduled/overdue/completed KPIs, server filters, 10/25/50/100 pagination, compact mobile cards, direct job records, and a scope-identical `.xlsx` export. Work orders still originate from maintenance requests so every contractor job retains its service context and audit trail.
- Authoritative contracts, maintenance closeouts, statements, and receipts are private PDF documents with bilingual Arabic shaping. Management can also download editable bilingual DOCX working copies for lease contracts and resolved maintenance closeouts; tenants receive only the authoritative PDF records. Reports export as real `.xlsx` workbooks. Dashboard, portfolio control, property details, reports, PDF/DOCX statements, and XLSX workbooks keep every financial position separated by currency; unlike currencies are never collapsed into a false total.
- Every authorized top-level property now has a dedicated bilingual Operating Report. It keeps hierarchy, occupancy, ownership, revenue, expenses, net position, maintenance, source queues, and genuine property-specific PDF/DOCX/XLSX downloads inside one building scope instead of returning users to the general report library. The editable Word and five-sheet Excel files include the complete scoped period, while browser cards remain intentionally bounded for speed.
- Owners, assigned managers, and superadmins have one authoritative bilingual Rent Roll. Rentable assets remain the source rows so vacancies cannot disappear; active tenancy, contract dates, rent, deposits, paid, outstanding, and overdue positions are searchable and filterable by portfolio, property, and occupancy state. Results use server pagination, preserve separate currency positions, and download under the identical authorization and filter scope. DOCX and XLSX contain the complete filtered schedule; the landscape PDF keeps complete totals but caps detail at 100 visibly disclosed rows so large estates remain reliable on shared hosting.
- Owners, assigned managers, and superadmins have one authoritative bilingual Arrears Aging schedule. Every overdue installment is grouped into 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, or over-90-day risk, keeps unlike currencies separate, and links directly to the exact tenant collection follow-up and lease. Portfolio, property, search, pagination, and manager-assignment scopes are identical across the responsive browser workspace and complete PDF, DOCX, and two-sheet XLSX downloads.
- Owners, assigned managers, and superadmins have one actionable Lease Expiry and Renewal schedule. The live queue keeps contact dates, days remaining, renewal state, replacement-contract links, outstanding positions, property scope, and direct lease actions together. Its current-snapshot PDF, DOCX, and XLSX downloads use the exact same search, portfolio, property, horizon, status, and authorization scope; Word and Excel contain the complete schedule while the shared-hosting-safe landscape PDF visibly caps detail at 100 rows without changing its totals.
- Every authorized tenant profile now opens a dedicated bilingual Account Statement. It keeps all scoped current and historical contracts, period installments, posted and void payment records, maintenance activity, and generated account documents in focused tabs, preserves separate currency positions instead of adding unlike currencies, and downloads as real PDF, DOCX, or XLSX files.
- The report Operations tab includes a property-scoped operational journal for new leases, posted payments and expenses, opened and resolved maintenance, and uploaded documents. The browser shows the latest 12 events while property and portfolio exports include the complete filtered period.
- Report periods support fixed dates plus rolling this-month, last-month, last-30-days, and year-to-date windows. Saved reports now have their own focused index, create, show, and edit cycle instead of sharing the analytics screen. Each saved-report record connects its exact date and property scope to ownership, legal actions, audit history, and direct PDF, DOCX, and XLSX outputs. Scope-aware cards open or export the report, authorized users can duplicate and edit reusable views, global views cannot retain misleading property filters, and rolling periods never freeze the dates from the day they were saved.
- Every report-library card now states the exact period, portfolio, and property scope it will apply. Property Operating Report cards expose PDF, DOCX, and XLSX directly, and their document drill-downs preserve the selected building hierarchy through the document register, count summaries, and workbook export instead of silently widening back to the full portfolio.
- English and Arabic administration, public content, RTL layout, editable wording, localized map data, and CMS content are supported.
- Resource indexes use server pagination, scoped search and filters, desktop tables, compact mobile cards, exports, and detail pages.
- Launch readiness is measured in `/system/readiness`: automatic environment, scheduler, queue, storage, private-document, and recent recovery-package checks are combined with auditable evidence for SMTP delivery, offsite backups, restore drills, legal approval, opening data, billing rules, retention, and role pilots. Scheduler readiness requires three distinct samples across at least 90 seconds, so a one-off deployment heartbeat cannot impersonate a working Hostinger cron.
- Backup Control at `/system/backups` creates one private compressed package containing a consistent MySQL snapshot, protected document storage, release identity, and SHA-256 checksums. Only superadmins can create, inspect, download, or prune packages; one run can be active at a time; the latest seven completed archives are retained by default; and a weekly queued backup is registered for Sunday 02:30. Same-server completion is measured automatically, while offsite protection and a real restore drill remain explicit evidence gates.
- Superadmins can download the exact selected Launch Readiness scope as localized PDF, DOCX, or XLSX evidence. The PDF and Word versions preserve measured checks, manual evidence, confirming actor, portfolio blockers, and business approvals; Excel separates Summary, Infrastructure, Evidence, and Portfolio into real workbook sheets instead of flattening the audit into one row dump.
- Launch readiness now distinguishes operational portfolios from showcase and archived data, opens the exact next form for each blocker, and never lets a showcase portfolio create a false setup-complete state. Every live portfolio detail and eligible owner/superadmin dashboard share one six-step setup path for activation, owner, manager, property, tenant, and first lease.
- Live portfolio setup is sequential rather than a wall of shortcuts. Only the first incomplete step is actionable; owner, manager, and building forms are locked to that portfolio, reject tampered portfolio IDs before writing, and return to the same setup checklist after creation. A completed checklist collapses to one compact status row and can be reopened on demand, while valuation, posted-revenue, and net-position cards open the exact portfolio-scoped property register, payment register, and operating statement.

## Deliberate MVP limits

- Payments are recorded manually; there is no payment gateway or bank reconciliation.
- Sign-off is a PDF upload/download workflow; there is no cryptographic e-signature engine.
- Finance is operational income, arrears, expenses, and net position, not double-entry accounting.
- Operational activity has a scoped in-app notification center. Email still depends on production SMTP, and SMS/WhatsApp integrations remain outside the MVP.
- Shared hosting is supported through database queues and cron; long-running workers, Redis, Horizon, and realtime sockets are intentionally excluded.
