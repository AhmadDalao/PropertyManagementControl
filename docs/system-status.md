## Working product scope

- Superadmin can manage platform users, portfolios, opening data, assets, CMS content, wording, showcase data, launch readiness, infrastructure settings, private system backups, email delivery, reports, media, and audit history. The command center also shows a bounded, deduplicated cross-portfolio activity feed that excludes showcase records and opens each affected record directly; complete history remains in Audit History.
- Owners manage their portfolio. Property managers manage only properties/buildings explicitly assigned by an owner, including their floors, units, tenants, leases, manual payments, maintenance, expenses, documents, and reports. A manager with no assignment receives no operational data.
- Tenants can see their active rental, contract period, paid and remaining amounts, PDF documents, payment history, and maintenance requests. They can attach private JPG, PNG, or WebP evidence when opening a request or add it later from the request record; owners and assigned managers see the same evidence without exposing it through the public media library.
- Tenants can open an owned payment and submit one genuine private PDF payment proof with an optional note. Owners and assigned managers review the proof inside a dedicated Evidence tab, rejection requires a reason, rejected evidence can be replaced, and accepted evidence locks further submission. Proof handling is deliberately separate from posting money: it never changes payment status, creates allocations, or impersonates the authoritative system receipt.
- Owners and managers can start one guided tenancy journey: create the tenant account, continue into a prefilled draft lease, then complete a record-backed move-in checklist for the contract PDF, signed PDF, activation, and opening payment.
- Owners and assigned managers can hand off portal access from a dedicated user/tenant page. The system creates a fresh 60-minute password-setup link, revokes older reset links, blocks inactive accounts and out-of-scope managers, records a token-free audit event, and lets the recipient choose a private password without the operator retaining one.
- Owners and managers close tenancies through a controlled move-out plan. Completion requires the agreed date, returned keys, a deposit decision, termination-notice PDF, move-out inspection PDF, and preserves any final debt before releasing the asset.
- Assets support property, building, floor, unit, and space hierarchies with ownership, management, valuation, occupancy, and map coordinates.
- Owners and superadmins can create a complete building, its floors, and numbered units or commercial spaces in one guided transaction. The builder previews the generated structure, enforces active portfolio/stakeholder boundaries, and leaves the single-record form available for custom additions.
- Owners and superadmins can onboard a real portfolio from `/opening-data` using one bilingual multi-sheet XLSX workbook. Uploading creates a private, expiring preview only; duplicate codes, references, hierarchy, tenancy, currency, dates, and payment conflicts must pass validation before one atomic import creates assets, tenants, leases, installments, payments, allocations, occupancy, and an audit event. Failed imports roll back completely, and imported tenant accounts require the controlled portal-access handoff before login.
- Owners, managers, and superadmins set one property scope through a searchable, portfolio-grouped sidebar picker and carry it through dashboards, operational directories, the map, reports, queues, and XLSX exports. It scales beyond native-select limits, is session-backed, excludes inactive or unauthorized roots, clears safely when access changes, and never appears in the tenant portal or unrelated detail/system pages.
- Owners, managers, and superadmins compare their complete accessible estate in `/portfolio-control`. The bilingual card workspace ranks all properties by operating pressure, supports search, portfolio and health filters, server pagination, and opens the focused dashboard, Action Center, reports, or property record without returning to a crowded asset register.
- Lease installments distinguish current arrears from future contract balance. Payment allocation and reversal use database transactions and row locks.
- Owners and managers control rent collection through an append-only follow-up workspace. Every open installment can record the contact method, outcome, assignee, promise-to-pay, next action, and permanent history; broken promises and overdue accounts without contact feed dashboards, reports, filters, lease history, and XLSX exports.
- Owners, managers, and superadmins have one property-scoped Action Center that merges collection follow-ups, maintenance, lease renewals, move-outs, and expiring documents into a prioritized daily queue. The default page is deliberately bounded to six compact decision cards, reducing the 390px seeded page from 5,934px to 2,916px while preserving tenant, property, portfolio, due date, status, amount, assignee, work-order, and direct-action context. It supports assignee, property, priority, and work-type filters plus a compact bilingual Daily Operations Brief in PDF, editable DOCX, and real XLSX formats. Every format uses the exact current filters and authorization scope, keeps unlike currencies separate, and excludes foreign properties; managers see only assigned property hierarchies and tenants cannot access it.
- Owners, assigned managers, and superadmins have one central bilingual Work Order register instead of hunting through maintenance requests or contractor profiles. Work orders still originate from maintenance requests so every contractor job retains its service context and audit trail.

## Deliberate MVP limits

- Payments are recorded manually; there is no payment gateway or bank reconciliation.
- Sign-off is a PDF upload/download workflow; there is no cryptographic e-signature engine.
- Finance is operational income, arrears, expenses, and net position, not double-entry accounting.
- Operational activity has a scoped in-app notification center. Email still depends on production SMTP, and SMS/WhatsApp integrations remain outside the MVP.
- Shared hosting is supported through database queues and cron; long-running workers, Redis, Horizon, and realtime sockets are intentionally excluded.
